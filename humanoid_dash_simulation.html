🧠 Why is 3D Humanoid RL Hard?

  • High Dimensionality: 20–30 continuous torque motors with non-linear contact dynamics.
  • The "Falling Trap": 99% of early random motions cause immediate faceplants within 0.2 seconds.
  • Sample Inefficiency: PPO needs 10,000,000+ physics steps to settle on periodic limit cycles (gait oscillations).
